## 1. Tanim, Bes! — AI Plant-Care Assistant

**Banner:** `public/images/linkedin-banners/tanimbes-linkedin-banner.jpg`

🌱 **Meet Tanim, Bes! — your AI plant tito, in your pocket.**

Ever tried Googling why your plant's leaves are turning yellow? You get 15 blog posts, a forum thread from 2012, and advice written like a chemistry textbook. For new plant owners, that's usually where the journey ends.

**That's the problem we set out to solve.**

Plant-care advice online is fragmented, overly technical, and intimidating for beginners. So we built Tanim, Bes! — a mobile app that replaces all that searching with one friendly conversation.

Instead of browsing, you just chat. In casual Taglish. Like asking a knowledgeable friend, not reading a manual.

**What it does:**

💬 24/7 AI plant-care assistant — diagnose problems, get care routines, and grow any plant with confidence

🌿 One-tap plant topics — a curated Home grid of plants and care actions (planting, watering, pests, sunlight) that preloads tailored prompts into the chat

🔊 Read-aloud replies — every answer can be spoken out loud with text-to-speech

🙌 Guest mode — instant access, no signup required

**By the numbers:**

✂️ Eliminates hours of digging through scattered blogs, forums, and search results — one chat replaces them all

📱 3 platforms (Android, iOS, Web) shipped from 1 Expo codebase

⏱️ ~3 months from idea to cross-platform release

🕐 24/7 availability — plant emergencies don't keep office hours

**Under the hood:**
A Botpress-backed chatbot integrated directly via REST (no webview), Supabase auth for persistent sessions, and a single Expo + React Native codebase — all wrapped in a polished "Emerald & Gold" theme.

Because growing plants shouldn't feel like studying for an exam. It should feel like chatting with a tito who knows his halaman. 🪴

## 2. Pickleball District — Club Operations Platform

**Banner:** `public/images/linkedin-banners/pickleball-district-linkedin-banner.jpg`

🏸 **The whiteboard queue, retired.**

Walk into any open-play pickleball club and you'll find the same system: a whiteboard. Names in a column, a staff member calling the next four, and everyone else standing around guessing how long the wait is.

It breaks the moment the club gets busy — turns get skipped, friends who came together get split across courts, private rentals live in a notebook that double-books as often as not, and nothing about who played survives the end of the session.

**That's the problem we set out to solve.**

We built Pickleball District — a platform that turns the whole club into one live system.

**What it does:**

📺 Live queue and court status — players hold a real position they can watch from the wall display or their phone

👥 Social matchmaking — follow friends, invite them to your court, claim an open court, or stack onto a short one

⏱️ Playing time as a balance — tracked in minutes players can top up, with each 20-minute rotation ending and re-queueing automatically

💳 VIP courts and coaching booked and paid online against real availability — a booking reference, not a line in a notebook

🖥️ One front-desk dashboard — walk-ins, match rotation, court management, and live counts at a glance

**By the numbers:**

✂️ Eliminates the whiteboard entirely — the queue is live, self-service, and visible to everyone

🚫 0 double-bookings — reservations are checked against real availability

🔄 20-minute rotations end and re-queue automatically, no staff intervention

📊 100% of matches, players, and transactions recorded — instead of erased at closing time

**Under the hood:**
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, and Tailwind CSS — with match duration, players per court, opening hours, and payment hold windows all configurable so the club tunes its own rules.

## 3. RideLink — Peer-to-Peer Mobility Marketplace

**Banner:** `public/images/linkedin-banners/ridelink-linkedin-banner.jpg`

🚗 **Rent anything. List anything.**

Most vehicle-rental apps are one-directional: a fleet owner lists, a customer books. But what if every user could be both?

**That's the problem we set out to solve.**

We built RideLink — a true two-sided marketplace where any user is simultaneously a renter and an owner, across cars, motorcycles, and heavy equipment.

That raised three hard problems at once: trust and safety for peer-to-peer key handovers, live accountability for vehicles out on rental, and doing it all without a custom server tier.

**What it does:**

🔁 Dual-role flows — one account fluidly switches between renting and listing, with multi-step registration and admin approval pipelines

🛡️ Trust built in — verified identities, owner approvals, moderation, and database-level authorization so no user ever sees another's data

📍 Battery-aware live GPS — tracking activates only during an active rental, writing live position and time-series trails, then shuts off when idle

📋 Full rental lifecycle — pending → approved → ongoing → completed

**By the numbers:**

✂️ Eliminates the one-directional rental model — every user is both sides of the marketplace

🔧 470+ commits across ~3 years of iterative development, including a full 2026 re-architecture

🖥️ 0 custom servers — fully serverless on Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage), with the database itself as the security boundary

🔐 Envelope encryption with keys held in device secure enclaves

📱 1 React Native codebase shipping to both iOS and Android

**Under the hood:**
Expo + React Native on a fully serverless Supabase stack, a unified NativeWind design system of color, shadow, and typography tokens, and an incremental TypeScript migration.

## 4. HRMIS — Government HR System

**Banner:** `public/images/linkedin-banners/hrmis-linkedin-banner.jpg`

🗂️ **Every HR record. One source of truth.**

Across government offices, HR data lived everywhere and nowhere: disconnected spreadsheets, paper 201 files, standalone systems that never spoke to one another. Records duplicated and inconsistent between departments. Information slow to retrieve, hard to audit, nearly impossible to report on across the workforce as a whole.

**That's the problem we set out to solve.**

We built HRMIS — a centralized Human Resource Management Information System that consolidates every office's HR data into a single, authoritative platform.

**What it does:**

📁 Employee 201 files, payroll, leave, attendance, and reporting — all in one place

🔐 Role-based access control — each office and HR officer sees exactly what they're permitted to

📋 Standardized workflows replacing manual paperwork

📊 Consolidated dashboards giving administrators a real-time view of the entire workforce

**By the numbers:**

✂️ Eliminates the scattered spreadsheet-and-paper trail — 1 platform replaces every office's separate records

⏱️ Built and delivered in 7 months

🔍 Audit-ready records with faster, workforce-wide reporting

## 5. NFA Trading — AgriTech & Government

**Banner:** `public/images/linkedin-banners/nfa-trading-linkedin-banner.jpg`

🌾 **Fairer bids for Filipino farmers.**

The people who grow the rice capture the least of its value. Selling through layers of middlemen left Filipino farmers with little leverage over the price of their own harvest — while the National Food Authority lacked a clear, real-time view of rice moving across the country.

**That's the problem we set out to solve.**

We built NFA Trading — a platform that puts farmers' harvests in front of traders directly, through an open bidding marketplace.

**What it does:**

🤝 Open bidding marketplace — farmers list their harvest, traders bid, growers secure higher and fairer returns

🇵🇭 Nationwide inventory tracking — a central dashboard lets the NFA monitor rice across the Philippines in real time

🔐 Role-based access — farmers, traders, and agency staff all work from the same trusted source of data

**By the numbers:**

✂️ Eliminates the layers of middlemen between farmers and buyers — bids go straight to the growers

📈 Higher, fairer returns through open competition instead of take-it-or-leave-it pricing

🗺️ Real-time visibility of rice supply nationwide, replacing fragmented reporting

⏱️ Built and delivered in 9 months

**Under the hood:**
Next.js, React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL behind a REST API — marketplace, bidding, and inventory systems on one platform.
